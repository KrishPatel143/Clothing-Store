**SLIDE 2**

### AI-Powered Body Scan — No Guesswork Sizing

Shoppers get their measurements in seconds using just their phone camera — no tape measure, no store visit, no manual entry. The scan runs entirely on the customer's device, so no private photos or videos are ever sent to our servers.

**KEY CUSTOMER ADVANTAGES**

**Instant, Private, Accurate**
Camera-based scan estimates chest, waist, hip, and shoulder in under a minute.

- Builds customer trust (privacy-first, nothing stored)
- Removes size-guessing at checkout
- Works on any smartphone — zero extra hardware
